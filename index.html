WHAT WE BUILD

Six ways to bring a character to life

Full costumes, single props, or just the parts your build is missing — each piece is modeled, printed, and finished specifically for you.

3D Printing

Props, parts, and hard-surface pieces printed to spec in a range of materials, from display-grade resin detail to durable wearable PLA+.

Full Costume Builds

Screen-accurate or wholly original, built from reference to final fitting — armor, fabric, and accessories as one cohesive piece.

Armor & Props

Helmets, gauntlets, weapons, and shields — sculpted for silhouette first, then engineered to actually survive a convention floor.

Custom Garments

Bespoke jackets, capes, and structured pieces cut and sewn to your measurements — the fabric half of a build done properly.

Painting & Finishing

Priming, airbrushing, weathering, and sealing — the pass that turns a raw print or a raw seam into something that reads as real.

Consultations

Not sure what's feasible on your budget or timeline? Send references and we'll map out the most realistic way to build it.

HOW A BUILD RUNS

From reference images to finished costume

Every commission runs through the same four stages, so you always know what's happening and when.

01

Send your references

Share the character, event date, and any reference art or photos you have. The more detail, the tighter the final fit to your vision.

02

We scope the build

You get a breakdown of materials, a fixed quote, and a delivery date — including which parts are printed versus sewn.

03

Printing & construction

Modeling, printing, sanding, sewing, and painting happen in-house, with progress photos sent at each major milestone.

04

Fitting & delivery

Final adjustments, a fit check where possible, then it ships or you collect it — ready to wear the same week.
